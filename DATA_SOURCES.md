## Research notes

Public research has also surfaced the June 2026 Eugene release announcement and related UTAU community posts. Telegram channel `@ilyaminineli` is an intended source for first-party history, but its full post history is not reliably exposed to the available public search interface. Telegram-only claims therefore remain research leads until the original post can be retrieved directly.

## Rule

Never infer technical metadata from a song, artwork, or another voicebank. Promote information to canonical catalog data only when an authoritative source supports it.
